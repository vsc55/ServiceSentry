{# Credentials section: the markup of its tab pane. A section of its own rather than a sub-tab of Infrastructure. It arrived there when the catalogue was reusable SSH identities, which really are a host concern — but half of it is now Entra ID app registrations with no host behind them at all, reached by tenant rather than over the network. Its two former neighbours, Servers and Clusters, are things you MONITOR; a credential is not monitored, it is the secret you reach other things with. And its consumers are spread across hosts, modules and providers alike, so hanging it off any one of them asserted a belonging that had stopped being true. Not under Access either: that is users, groups, roles and sessions — who may enter the panel. These are machine identities the panel uses on its way out. Full-bleed with a flush card (see createListTable's cardClass), like History: no card chrome, filling the whole area, and eating the shell's top padding so the accent strip sits flush against the breadcrumb. #}
