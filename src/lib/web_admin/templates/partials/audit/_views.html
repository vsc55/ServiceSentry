The log is a list of lines and the table reads it one line at a time, which is the right shape for "what happened at 14:32" and the wrong one for every question that is about the log as a whole. Two of those matter enough to deserve their own view: * WHO has been active. The table shows every line by every actor and never a per-actor summary, so "an account nobody uses did forty things last night" is invisible unless you already suspected it and filtered by that user — which means you have to know the answer before you can ask the question. * WHEN it happened. Access at 03:00 on a Sunday and access at 11:00 on a Tuesday read identically in a list sorted by time; side by side on a day × hour grid, one of them is a shape you notice from across the room. Those two are SUMMARIES: they describe a set, so they are computed over everything the filters left standing rather than over the page on screen, and they are not paginated — page 2 of a summary is not a thing. The two list views (table and timeline) stay paginated, because they are the log itself.
