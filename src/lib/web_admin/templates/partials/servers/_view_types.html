{# ================================================================ Devices — by type: a rail of what the fleet is MADE OF. The other four views answer questions about state — how is it, what is watched, what is each one. This one answers a question about composition: what is in here, and how much of each. A table sorted by type can be paged through until you have the answer; a rail states it before you read a row, and picking one is how you get "show me the switches". Three things it does deliberately: * **Only the types that are present.** Eight empty rows for the kinds of box this site does not have would be eight rows of noise — an absent printer is not news. But "unclassified" IS news when it is not empty: those are the devices somebody added in a hurry, and this is where you find them. * **The catalogue's order, never the count.** A rail that reshuffled as devices came and went would move the thing you were about to click. Which type is biggest is what the numbers are for. * **A summary**, like Status and Coverage: it is handed everything the filters left standing rather than the page on screen. Counts over one page would change as you paged through them, and a rail whose counts do that is worse than no counts.
