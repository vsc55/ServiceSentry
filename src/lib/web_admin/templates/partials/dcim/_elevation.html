{# ================================================================ The rack, drawn: front and rear, U by U, coloured by what the probes already know. This is the point of the whole section. A drawing that shows one face at a time makes the reader hold the other one in their head, which is the thing they came here to stop doing. **On the shared canvas** (`infra/_canvas.html`), so panning, zooming, fitting and — the one that pays for itself immediately — **exporting to PNG or SVG** are the same code the two maps use.
